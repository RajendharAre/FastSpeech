- **2026-11-05:** English training resumed from checkpoint `30000.pth.tar`. The user-provided final log reports step 60,000, validation loss 4.0910 and a saved `checkpoints/60000.pth.tar`. Durable copies of the selected checkpoint and inference outputs, plus listening review, remain to be confirmed.
- **2026-11-05:** English inference completed in Kaggle using checkpoint step 35,000. The run wrote WAVs for E1, E2a, E2b, E3 and E4, plus duration, pitch, energy plots and JSON prediction arrays for each sentence under `Results/English/`. User-provided console output confirms those writes; audio listening review and transfer of outputs into the local deliverable tree are pending.
- **2026-11-05:** The user reports that all five English outputs sound incorrect/unintelligible; E2a's speech-recognition transcript was rendered in Telugu script. Inspection of the E2a inference log and JSON confirms the input was converted to English ARPAbet, `language` is `en`, and checkpoint step 35,000 was used. The output files are non-empty, mono 22,050 Hz, and duration/mel-frame counts agree. This does not independently verify perceived audio quality or identify a single root cause. Report section 8 records the finding with caveats; investigate speaker ID (`0` remains a placeholder) and training/data generalisation before claiming a fix.
- **2026-11-05:** Kaggle reports `speakers.json` as `{"female": 0, "male": 1}` and English inference `speaker_id: 0`; this confirms inference selected the female speaker ID, removing a speaker-ID mismatch as the current suspected cause. Report section 8 updated; model/data generalisation remains under investigation.
- **2026-11-05:** Added the resumed-run English training and validation loss plots under `Results/English/` and embedded them in report section 7. The plots cover approximately steps 30,000–60,000 only; the earlier shared logs are excluded from a stitched full-run analysis because two processes wrote to them. The PDF renderer now embeds local Markdown images and fails explicitly for missing image paths. Verified with `python -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -p test_render_report_pdf.py -v` (9 passed) and rebuilt `Report/Technical_Report.pdf` with `--check` (no problems; 220 Telugu code points).
- **2026-11-05:** English and Telugu inference now load trusted project checkpoints through `utils.train_utils.load_checkpoint` rather than calling `torch.load` directly. This preserves the explicit `weights_only=False` required by checkpoints containing the Python/NumPy RNG state on the measured PyTorch 2.11 environment.
- **2026-11-05:** English and Telugu HiFi-GAN loading now passes `map_location=device` to `torch.load`, allowing inference on CPU when the vocoder weights were saved with CUDA device tags.
- **2026-11-06:** Measured why the English outputs are unintelligible, from the five saved WAVs and variance JSONs only (no data or GPU needed). Durations and lengths are correct (predicted symbol durations sum exactly to the mel-frame count; 5.9-6.7 frames per symbol = 69-79 ms). The waveform is tonal, not noise (spectral flatness 0.007-0.013, no clipping, correct mono 22,050 Hz). The variance adaptor collapses to the mean: predicted normalised pitch std 0.24-0.40 and energy std 0.32-0.70 against a unit-variance target, with negative predicted energy means matching the low WAV RMS (0.033-0.077). The loss curves agree - validation pitch MSE 0.68 and energy MSE 1.15 against a predict-the-mean baseline of 1.0, while training MSE is 0.02-0.08; validation mel L1 1.05 vs training 0.6. Conclusion: 213 epochs of 4.84 h through 35 M parameters memorised the training split instead of generalising, which is consistent with validation loss bottoming near step 7,000. Speaker-ID, frontend-language, file-shape and length-regulator explanations are ruled out by these measurements. The vocoder itself is still untested against a ground-truth mel; that test is item 4 of `docs/AI_HANDOFF.md`.
